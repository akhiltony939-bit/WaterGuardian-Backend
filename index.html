About WaterGuardian-X

WaterGuardian-X is a Smart Water Management System designed to connect citizens and authorities through a digital complaint management platform. It helps in reporting water issues, tracking solutions, and improving water conservation using technology.

🎯 Objective

Reduce water wastage and provide faster complaint resolution.

💡 Solution

A centralized portal for citizens and administrators.

🌍 Impact

Promotes awareness and responsible water usage.
